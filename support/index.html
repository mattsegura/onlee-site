Subscriptions

Onlee Plus is billed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the paywall in Onlee and tap Restore.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Everything Onlee stores stays on your device. Settings → Reset all progress erases it. See the privacy policy.
