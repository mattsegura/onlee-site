Onlee Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Onlee is a language-learning app published by ZAPR LABS LLC ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

What stays on your device Your learning progress, streaks, saved sentences, settings and reminder schedule are stored only on your device. Onlee has no accounts and no server, and we don't receive any of this data.

Microphone and speech recognition When you use "Say it", Onlee records your voice to score your pronunciation. Recognition runs on your device when your device supports it. Otherwise Apple's speech recognition service processes the audio under Apple's privacy policy. Onlee doesn't store or send recordings anywhere else.

Purchases Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app checks your subscription status with the App Store.

Tracking, ads and analytics Onlee has no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.

Children Onlee isn't directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data Settings → Reset all progress permanently erases everything Onlee stored on your device. Deleting the app does the same. To stop paying, also cancel your subscription in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Changes If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
